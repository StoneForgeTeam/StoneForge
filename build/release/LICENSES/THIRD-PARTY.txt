StoneForge's licences
=====================

StoneForge (StoneForge.API, StoneForge.Loader, StoneForge.Patcher and the rest) - MIT licence (StoneForge-MIT.txt),
except:
- StoneForge.MslHost (dotnet\patcher\msl\StoneForge.MslHost.*) and ModShardLauncher 0.13.2.0's own
  ModShardLauncher.dll beside it - GNU GPL-3.0 (MSL-GPL-3.0.txt). Provenance and checksums: MSL-SOURCE.md.
  Helper dependencies: Newtonsoft.Json and System.Drawing.Common (MIT); Serilog and its Console sink (Apache-2.0).
- StoneForge.Bridge (aurie\StoneForge.Bridge.dll) - GNU AGPL-3.0 (YYToolkit-AGPL-3.0.txt): it includes YYToolkit's
  code.

It ships with these components, each under its own licence:

- Aurie (AurieCore.dll, dotnet\patcher\AuriePatcher.exe) - GNU AGPL-3.0 (Aurie-AGPL-3.0.txt). Built from
  https://github.com/AurieFramework/Aurie at the commit in Aurie-SOURCE.md, with Aurie-stoneforge.patch applied
  (modules load from <game>\aurie instead of <game>\mods\aurie).
- YYToolkit (aurie\YYToolkit.dll) - GNU AGPL-3.0 (YYToolkit-AGPL-3.0.txt). Built, unmodified, from
  https://github.com/AurieFramework/YYToolkit at the commit in YYToolkit-SOURCE.md.
- Roslyn, the .NET compiler platform (dotnet\Microsoft.CodeAnalysis*.dll, and in dotnet\patcher\) - MIT licence.
- UndertaleModLib 0.9.2.0 (dotnet\patcher\) - GNU GPL-3.0 (UndertaleModLib-GPL-3.0.txt).
- Underanalyzer (dotnet\patcher\) - MPL-2.0 (Underanalyzer-MPL-2.0.txt).
  Both are unmodified binaries; exact source revisions and checksums are in UndertaleModLib-SOURCE.md.

The AGPL and GPL require the source of these components, and of works built on them, to be available to anyone
who receives them: StoneForge's source is published with it, and Aurie's and YYToolkit's are their upstream
repositories at the commits named above, plus Aurie-stoneforge.patch.
